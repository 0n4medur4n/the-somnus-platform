## 1. What The Somnus is
The Somnus offers Morpheo, a tool for an initial orientation about sleep. It helps you organise what is happening and prepare a visit with a professional.

## 2. What it is not
Morpheo does not diagnose, does not rule out illness, does not indicate treatment and does not replace a healthcare professional. Fixed rules calculate the result. The report you see at the end of the current interview is made of sentences that were already approved. If you think you are facing an emergency, call 112 or go to an emergency department.

## 3. Who can use it
You must be 18 or older to open an account. If you use Morpheo about a minor, you declare that you are their parent or legal guardian. Minors never have an account of their own.

## 4. Your account
You sign in with a single-use link that we send to your email; there is no password. You are responsible for keeping your email secure. You can delete your account at any time. Doing so deletes your saved assessments, the reports of those assessments stored under your name, and your profile photo. The case record of each interview is kept for 24 months and is no longer linked to you. The privacy policy says which other data is deleted, which is kept, and which other services receive data.

## 5. Proper use
You must not use the service for purposes other than sleep orientation, or try to access other people's data or alter the running of the platform.

## 6. Changes to these terms
If we change these terms we will publish a new version. We never modify a version already accepted: each version is kept as you accepted it.

## 7. The service
The service is called The Somnus.
