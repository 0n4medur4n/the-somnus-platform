## Consent to the processing of health data

By accepting this document you authorise The Somnus to process the answers you give in the Morpheo sleep assessment, and the voice if you dictate. These answers are health data.

## What data
Your answers to the questions, including the text you write, and the guidance result calculated from them. If you dictate, the audio as well. If the assessment is about a minor in your care, the answers refer to that minor. We also keep a record of the assessment under a random case number, which you will see next to your result: it contains what you were asked, what you answered and what you were shown, and it does not include your name, your email or your account.

## What for
To calculate your guidance, show it to you and save it in your account so you can look at it later. Fixed rules calculate the result. No model decides your level of care. The report you see at the end of the current interview is made of sentences that were already approved. A model does not write it.

## Who else processes it
If you dictate, the browser sends the audio to AssemblyAI, at streaming.eu.assemblyai.com, only to turn it into text. The audio does not pass through our servers and we do not keep it. The text comes back to the box and you can correct it before continuing. Your written answers are not sent to OpenAI: the interview service is configured with that extraction off and with no key. There is another route, which the current interview does not use, by which the report service can send OpenAI (https://api.openai.com/v1) the level of care, the patterns and the protocol text, in order to write or translate an explanation. That call does not include your name, your email, your identifier or your free-text answers.

## What we do not do
We do not use your answers for advertising and we do not show them to any professional without your express permission. The platform's super administration may open the case record, without your name, email or account, with a written justification that is recorded, to deal with a claim or an audit.

## How long
For as long as you keep your account. If you do not save an assessment to an account, it is deleted after 30 days. The case record is kept for 24 months from the day the assessment started and is then deleted. It is kept even if your assessment or your account is deleted, but it is then no longer linked to you.

## Your right to withdraw it
This consent is voluntary. You can withdraw it at any time from “My consents”. Withdrawing it does not affect processing carried out before. Without this consent the application does not start the assessment. Withdrawing it does not delete the case record before that 24-month period ends.

## Limitations
Morpheo is not a diagnosis. In an emergency, call 112.
